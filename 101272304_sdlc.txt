## Software development life cycle or (SDLC) includes planning,development, testing, deployment, and maintinence. 

## Devops supports these stages by improving collaboration between development and operations.

## through autopmation and regular feedback, teams can identify problems early and reliable software.

## During development and testing, Devops helps teams work together and check changes regularly.

## Version control keeps a history of changes, while automated test can detect errors before software is released.
## this allows developers to fix problems earlier and reduce chance of introducing bugs into the main branch


## During deployment and maintinence, Devops helps teams release updates consistently through automation. 
## after deployment monitoring and user feedback helps teams identify problems and plan improvements.
# This ongoing process helps keeps software reliable and responsive to users needs.

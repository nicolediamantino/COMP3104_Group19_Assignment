George Brown College,

As a busy mom, what can I say about George Brown College?
For me it is more than just a College. It's a place that gives people like me, the opportunity to continue their education 
while balancing family responsibilitis or even  work as the same time.

George Brown College is a public College in Toronto, in Ontario, Offering so many different programs in areas such as: 
Technology, business, healthcare, and design.
One interesting fact I discovered is that its Casa Loma campus is located on the traditional lands of Indigenous Peoples, 
and I appreciate how GBC recognizes and respects their history, values and culture.

Besides that, what attracted me to GBC was its online Computer Programming and Analysis (T-197)program. As a mother, this flexibility is
very important because it allows me to study while being available for my daughter, especially when she is sick or when I need 
to attend some meetings at her school.

Throughout my time at GBC, I came to realize that many other students, including mature student and mothers like me, are also working 
towards a better future. For many of us, this program is more than just an education. It's an opportunity to build a new career,
 change our lives and follow dreams that we once only dared to imagine.
